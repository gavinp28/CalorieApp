Calorie Guesser

How to play

Guess the calories in the food shown. In Full plate, guess the total for everything on the plate, then see each food's calories at the end. You get 5 guesses.

  • Within 5% counts as a win.
  • Within 10% means you're very close.
  • Within 25% means you're warm.
  • Every guess tells you to go higher or lower, and the bar narrows to where the answer must be.

Both modes get a new puzzle every day at midnight. The 5 most recent past puzzles in each archive are free.
